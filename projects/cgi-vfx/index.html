CGI / VFX

3D generalist · compositor · visual effects

Since 2018 I have worked as a 3D artist, 3D generalist and compositor at Mask Studio and Yuna Studio, on Iranian feature films, series and short films. The visual effects of Takhti (2019) and Day Zero (2020), which I worked on as a 3D generalist, were nominated for Best Visual Effects at the Fajr International Film Festival, and the short film Orthodontics (2021) was a Festival de Cannes short film selection. I work mainly in Cinema 4D, Houdini, Redshift and After Effects.
