Assignment 2 — BodyGame [A.I Assisted]

your body makes the nodes, the nodes make the geometry · ml5.js + p5.js

Allow the camera, then step in front of it. The places where you are make glowing nodes, and the nodes join into a shape.

  • Make exactly the right number of nodes and hold still: 3 triangle, 4 tetrahedron, 6 hexagon (keep holding and it lifts into an octahedron), 8 cube, 12 icosahedron, 13 Metatron's cube, 20 dodecahedron, 30 or more E8.
  • Step closer or open your arms for more nodes. Step back for fewer.
  • Harder geometries give more points, and finding a new one gives double.
  • No camera? Hold the mouse down and paint nodes. Up and down arrows change how far apart the nodes are. R starts again.
